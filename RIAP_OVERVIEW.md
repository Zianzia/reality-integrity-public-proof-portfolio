# RIAP — Reality Integrity Action Protocol

## Public problem statement

RIAP addresses an evidence gap around consequential AI and software actions: preserving a portable, independently verifiable account of why an action was authorized and whether the surviving evidence is sufficient to reconstruct that action later.

## Publicly stated objective

RIAP focuses on producing verifiable evidence around consequential actions, including:

- proposed action;
- governing authority;
- approval or control state;
- policy decision;
- execution result;
- integrity evidence;
- receipt verification.

## Demonstrated implementation areas

Private development work includes deterministic policy evaluation, canonicalization, signed receipts, verification, nonce/replay handling, authenticated status concepts, action-gateway design, human verification interfaces, and adversarial test scenarios. These implementation areas should not be read as claims that every component is production-deployed or independently certified.

## Public claims boundary

RIAP does **not** prove that an underlying decision was wise, lawful, fair, ethical, safe, or factually correct merely because the action is evidenced.

Evidence integrity and decision correctness are separate questions.

RIAP should not be represented publicly as a regulatory certification, legal conclusion, universal cybersecurity guarantee, or proof that every external action can be observed.

## Public shorthand

> Portable evidence of what was authorized, what happened, and whether the record can still be trusted.
