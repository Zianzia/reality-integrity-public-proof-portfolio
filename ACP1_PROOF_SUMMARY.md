# ACP-1 — Proof of Control

## What ACP-1 demonstrates

ACP-1 is an exercised proof-of-control prototype in a controlled simulation.

The public claim is intentionally narrow:

> MOMMA ACP-1 has an exercised proof-of-control prototype in a controlled simulation.

Within that exercised simulation, the prototype demonstrates control boundaries around an action rather than treating model output as unrestricted authority.

## What ACP-1 does not demonstrate

ACP-1 should not be represented as:

- a production deployment;
- proof of universal autonomous control;
- proof of legal compliance;
- proof that compromise is impossible;
- proof that every external system will honor the same controls;
- a substitute for independent operational or security review.

## Public shorthand

> ACP-1 demonstrates proof-of-control behavior in a controlled simulation.
