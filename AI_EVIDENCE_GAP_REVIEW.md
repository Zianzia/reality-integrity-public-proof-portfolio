## Core question

> If your AI made the wrong decision tomorrow, could you prove what it saw, what it was allowed to do, what it actually did, who approved it, and whether the surviving evidence could be trusted?

## What the review examines

- Authority
- Evidence
- Integrity
- Failure control
- Recovery

- System Evidence Map
- Authority Boundary Assessment
- Evidence Gap Register
- Five controlled failure tests
- Prioritized remediation plan
- Executive finding

Possible executive findings:

- ADEQUATE FOR CURRENT USE
- GAPS PRESENT
- MATERIAL CONTROL GAPS
- EVIDENCE FAILURE

## Claims boundary

The AI Evidence Gap Review is a technical and operational risk assessment. It is not legal advice, regulatory certification, a cybersecurity penetration test, SOC 2 examination, ISO certification, financial audit, or guarantee that an AI system cannot fail or be compromised.
