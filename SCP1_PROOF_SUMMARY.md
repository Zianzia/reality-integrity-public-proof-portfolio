# SCP-1 — Sovereign Compute Proof

## Public claim

> MOMMA SCP-1 v0.1 has a locally exercised proof harness for bounded external-compute delegation, retained decision authority, provider substitution/fallback, signed Celestial Nexus-style receipts, Merkle commitment, tamper rejection, detached verification, and non-submitting network-readiness planning. Live Nosana and Solana network execution remain unproven.

## Core objective

SCP-1 asks whether MOMMA can delegate bounded computation to an external compute provider without surrendering protected state or governing authority, while preserving independent evidence of the resulting action.

## Architectural principle

> Computation may leave MOMMA. Authority does not.

> Evidence may become public. Protected content does not have to.

## Demonstrated locally

The proof harness has exercised:

- bounded compute envelopes;
- retained decision authority;
- provider substitution/fallback;
- signed receipts;
- Merkle commitment;
- tamper rejection;
- detached verification;
- non-submitting network-readiness planning.

## Not yet demonstrated

- live Nosana execution;
- live Solana anchoring;
- production key custody;
- production-scale confidential-compute guarantees;
- universal provider trust or availability.

## Relationship to lineage

ACP-1 establishes the proof-of-control lineage in controlled simulation. SCP-1 extends that lineage into locally exercised bounded external-compute delegation. Celestial Nexus is intended to provide independently verifiable witnessing of resulting actions.
