# Public Claims Boundary

This portfolio uses an evidence-first claims posture.

## Permitted claims

Public materials may describe:

- locally exercised proof harnesses;
- controlled simulations;
- deterministic policy and receipt architectures;
- signed evidence and detached verification where actually exercised;
- bounded assessment and governance services;
- documented limitations and unresolved deployment gates.

## Prohibited or premature claims

Do not claim that current systems:

- guarantee AI safety;
- guarantee cybersecurity;
- guarantee legal compliance;
- prove that an underlying decision was wise, fair, lawful, ethical, or factually correct merely because evidence exists;
- detect every clone, fetch, exfiltration, or theft;
- identify every attacker;
- automatically recover from every incident;
- provide legally admissible proof in every jurisdiction;
- continuously monitor GitHub in real time unless the live continuous observer is actually operational;
- have completed live Nosana or Solana execution when those network proofs remain unperformed;
- provide production signing-key custody where that boundary is not yet implemented.

## Evidence principle

> Evidence can establish that records, actions, approvals, or transitions occurred or remained intact. Evidence alone does not establish the wisdom, legality, safety, fairness, or correctness of the underlying decision.

## Repository principle

Public summaries are derivative artifacts. A public summary does not authorize changing any private source repository to public visibility.
